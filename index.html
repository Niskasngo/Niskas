Development with dignity, participation and local ownership.

NISKAS is a voluntary agency registered under the Society Registration Act 1860. We work to promote education, health and environmentally sound, sustainable agro-based income-generating activities while strengthening village-level youth and traditional institutions.

Our approach is based on partnership and developmental initiatives rather than charity. We encourage local contribution, participation and stronger community institutions so that people can build lasting solutions for themselves.
