ABOUT NISKAS

Development through partnership, participation and self-reliance.

NISKAS is a voluntary agency registered under the Society Registration Act, 1860. The organisation works with weaker and disadvantaged sections through developmental initiatives rather than charity.

Our approach encourages local contribution, strengthens community-based organisations and develops intrinsic skills and managerial capabilities.
